Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Copiamo™ is a clipboard history app for Windows made by Jatin Jain. This policy covers the Copiamo app and this website.

The short version

Copiamo saves what you copy, cut and paste so you can find it again, and it keeps all of it on your own PC. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing you copy is sent to us or anyone else.

What Copiamo saves, and why

WhatWhy
The content of each copy or cut: text, links, images, and the paths (not contents) of copied filesSo you can see and copy it again
The app it came from (name and program location), its window title, and for browsers the web address of the pageTo show and filter by source
The app you pasted into and its window titleTo show and filter by destination
Date and time, and whether it was a copy or a cutTo sort and filter your trail
The prompts you write, their names and groupsSo your prompts are there next time
Your settings (theme, passwords, default clipboard)To remember your choices

All of this is stored in Copiamo's own app data folder on your PC. It never leaves your computer. Copiamo makes no internet connections of its own.

Passwords and private copies

  • Password managers such as 1Password, Bitwarden and KeePass mark what they copy as private. Copiamo respects that mark, and also skips copies from known password manager apps.
  • By default these copies are not saved at all: Copiamo doesn't read them and only notes, in your trail, that a clip from that app wasn't recorded.
  • If you turn on Store passwords, they are saved on your PC and shown as •••••• until you choose to reveal one.
  • Copiamo can't tell a password apart from other text when it comes from anywhere else (for example, typed into a document and copied). Those copies are saved like any other text.

Keyboard

To tell a cut from a copy and to know where you paste, Copiamo watches for the copy, cut and paste shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V, Shift+Insert, Ctrl+Insert, Shift+Delete). Every other key is ignored on the spot: nothing you type is recorded or stored.

Sending prompts to AI assistants

  • When you press Send to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, Copiamo looks, on your PC only, at the names and titles of open windows and running programs to find where that assistant is open. This list is used once and not saved.
  • Copiamo then copies your prompt and pastes it into the window you pick, or opens the app or website you choose. It never presses Enter: nothing is sent until you send it yourself.
  • Once you send a prompt, the assistant's own privacy policy applies (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for ChatGPT, Google for Gemini). Copiamo has no connection to these services.

Your control

  • Delete one clip: hover it and press the bin icon.
  • Delete everything: Settings › Clear history. Prompts are kept until you delete them.
  • Stop recording: quit Copiamo from its tray icon. If it's your default clipboard, unset that in Settings (or turn it off in Windows Settings › Apps › Startup) to stop it starting with Windows.
  • Remove all data: uninstalling Copiamo deletes its app data folder, including your history and prompts.

What Copiamo does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or personal information.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, cookies or crash reporting.
  • No uploading or syncing of your clipboard or prompts.
  • No data is sold or shared, because none is collected.

Microsoft Store

Copiamo is installed and updated through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft may collect data about installs, purchases and app health under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Copiamo receives no personal information from this.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log basic visit information (such as IP addresses) for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Copiamo collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if an optional feature such as sync needs the internet), this page will be updated before the change ships, and the feature will be off unless you turn it on.
